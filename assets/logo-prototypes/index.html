Emberveil — logo prototypes, round 3

Four marks carried through: the Emberrise and Veiled Sun baselines, plus Bloom and Drift. Each family gets three further passes built on the survivor rather than on the baseline. Every mark is shown on both Emberveil surfaces at 88px, down a size ramp to 16px, and on a rounded app tile.

Emberrise

A sun crossing a horizon. Round three keeps the flat baseline and the radial bloom, then pushes the bloom three ways: through a broken horizon, out into a wide halo, and down onto a lava lake.
